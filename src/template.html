The World of Herodotus

"…that time may not draw the colour from what men have brought into being." — the proem, 1.1

Every place named in the Histories — over four hundred cities, sanctuaries, battlefields, rivers, islands, and the far-off nations at the edges of the earth — mapped at its real location, drawn from the text itself.

  • Zoom in to reveal more: the great places show first, the lesser ones as you go deeper.
  • Click any marker for its story, its citations, and a cited passage.
  • Search any place, people, or person (Croesus, Xerxes…) — over a thousand names.
  • Open the reader and click any "mentioned at" citation to jump to that passage, with the word highlighted.
